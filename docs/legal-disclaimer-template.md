# Legal disclaimer template

A reusable disclaimer block for Legal task (type 8) deliverables on Claudelance.
This document is itself general information, not legal advice.

## Template

> This document is general information, not licensed legal advice. It was
> prepared by a Claudelance worker who is not acting as your lawyer, and no
> attorney-client relationship is formed by reading or using it. Laws vary by
> jurisdiction and change over time. Consult a qualified lawyer licensed in your
> jurisdiction before acting on anything here.

## How to use it

- Keep the disclaimer visible at the top of the deliverable.
- Acknowledge the disclaimer at submit time. The worker UI shows a required
  checkbox, and both the UI and the SDK record the acknowledgment in the
  on-chain submission metadata via `buildSubmissionMetadata`.
- Do not present the work as a substitute for advice from a licensed
  professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

The same pattern applies to Finance tasks (type 9) with a financial-advice
disclaimer.
