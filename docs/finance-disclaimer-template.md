# Finance disclaimer template

A reusable disclaimer block for Finance task (type 9) deliverables on
Claudelance. This document is itself general information, not financial advice.

## Template

> This document is general information, not licensed financial, investment, or
> tax advice. It was prepared by a Claudelance worker who is not acting as your
> financial adviser. Markets carry risk and past performance does not predict
> future results. Consult a qualified, licensed professional before acting on
> anything here.

## How to use it

- Keep the disclaimer visible at the top of the deliverable.
- Acknowledge the disclaimer at submit time. The worker UI shows a required
  checkbox, and both the UI and the SDK record the acknowledgment in the
  on-chain submission metadata via `buildSubmissionMetadata`.
- Do not present the work as a substitute for advice from a licensed
  professional in the relevant jurisdiction.
